BAO CAO GIAI DOAN 3 - TICH HOP IDENTITY

1. Muc tieu
Tich hop ASP.NET Core Identity va cookie authorization vao MVC/EF Core hien co, giu nguyen cac bang nghiep vu va tao schema Identity bang migration additive.

2. Phan tich
ApplicationDbContext ke thua IdentityDbContext<IdentityUser> va giu nguyen DbSet cua cac thuc the nghiep vu. Identity duoc dang ky voi EF stores, token providers, cookie login/access-denied paths, confirmed account, unique email, password policy va lockout. Authentication middleware dat truoc authorization.

7. Thay doi database
- Tao backup SQLite nhat quan truoc migration tai session files (khong nam trong repository).
- Ap dung AddIdentitySchema thanh cong tren App.db.
- So dong bang cu van giu nguyen: Students 11, Faculties 3, Employees 0, Lecturers 0.
- Cac bang AspNetUsers/AspNetRoles hien trong database va rong; seed roles/users se duoc thuc hien o giai doan 6.
- PRAGMA foreign_key_check khong co vi pham.
- Phat hien __EFMigrationsLock co dong cu tu 2026-10-22 lam EF Core 10 cho doi. Sau khi xac nhan khong co tien trinh migration khac, da xoa dung dong lock cu (chi metadata lock), sau do migration chay thanh cong; lock hien rong.

10. Ket qua kiem thu

11. Cac loi da gap
Lan database update dau tien dung tai migration lock cu con lai tu thang 10/2026; khong co migration nao duoc ap dung trong lan do.

12. Cach khac phuc
Da dung tien trinh EF dang treo, kiem tra row lock co timestamp cu va khong con tien trinh update nao chay, xoa row metadata lock cu, sau do chay lai database update va xac nhan thanh cong.

13. Luu y cho buoc tiep theo
Identity schema hien tai da co tren App.db. Cac giai doan tiep theo se them Account flows, email sender, role seeding va authorization. Khong duoc xoa cac bang Identity hoac dung EnsureCreated.
