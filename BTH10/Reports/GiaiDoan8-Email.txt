BAO CAO GIAI DOAN 8 - EMAIL

1. Muc tieu
Kiem tra Register, ConfirmEmail, ForgotPassword, ResetPassword va trang thai cau hinh SMTP.

2. Phan tich hien trang
- IEmailSender duoc dang ky qua DI.
- DevelopmentEmailSender ghi email to/subject/message vao log va khong gui ra ben ngoai.
- SmtpEmailSender doc SmtpOptions tu appsettings/environment.
- appsettings.json co Host, SenderEmail, UserName, Password rong.
- Register va ForgotPassword tao callback URL bang Url.ActionLink.
- ConfirmEmail dung UserManager ConfirmEmailAsync.
- ResetPassword dung UserManager GeneratePasswordResetTokenAsync va ResetPasswordAsync.

3. Phuong an da de xuat
- Phuong an A: giu Development sender cho Development va SMTP sender cho moi truong Production.
- Phuong an B: luon dung SMTP bang cac thong tin hard-code.
- Phuong an C: tat email va bo callback flow.

4. Phuong an duoc lua chon
Phuong an A. Duong email da tach DI va khong hard-code SMTP; Production se can cau hinh thong tin that.

5. Cac file da sua
Khong sua ma nguon trong giai doan nay.

6. Cac file moi tao
Khong co file ma nguon moi.

7. Cac package da cai
Khong cai package moi.

8. Migration da tao
Khong tao migration moi.

9. Thay doi Database
Khong thay doi schema.
- Tao va dọn tai khoan smoke email.smoke@example.local sau kiem thu.

10. Cac buoc thuc hien
- Xac nhan DI chon DevelopmentEmailSender trong Development.
- Dang ky tai khoan smoke.
- Doc link ConfirmEmail tu log.
- Mo link va ConfirmEmailAsync thanh cong.
- Goi ForgotPassword cho tai khoan da xac nhan.
- Doc link ResetPassword tu log.
- Mo link, dat mat khau moi va login lai thanh cong.
- Kiem tra appsettings SMTP rong.

11. Ket qua Build
- dotnet build thanh cong.
- 0 error.
- Con 2 warning NU1901 ve NuGet.

12. Ket qua kiem thu
- DevelopmentEmailSender ghi email va callback day du.
- Register confirmation page hoat dong.
- ConfirmEmail callback hoat dong va xac nhan tai khoan.
- ForgotPassword callback hoat dong va gui email gia lap qua log.
- ResetPassword callback hoat dong.
- Dat lai mat khau va dang nhap bang mat khau moi thanh cong.
- SMTP thuc te CHUA duoc xac minh vi Host/SenderEmail/UserName/Password rong.

13. Cac loi phat hien
- Khong co loi build/runtime trong luong callback.
- Khong co dich vu SMTP that de gui email ra internet.

14. Cach khac phuc
- Production can dat Smtp:Host, Smtp:Port, Smtp:SenderEmail, Smtp:SenderName, Smtp:EnableSsl va neu can Smtp:UserName/Smtp:Password qua environment variables hoac User Secrets.
- Khong commit Smtp:Password vao repository.

15. Luu y cho giai doan tiep theo
- Giai doan 9 can kiem thu toan bo build, auth, authorization, CRUD, AJAX va database mot lan cuoi.
- Can kiem thu Account confirmation bang giao dien that neu co SMTP/Sandbox.
- Can xac minh password nu seeding khong bi de lo trong appsettings Development neu da duoc reuse.
