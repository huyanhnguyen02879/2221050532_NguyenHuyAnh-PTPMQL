1. Muc tieu
Thay phan quyen role hard-code bang phan quyen dong theo Controller va Action, co ma tran cap/thu hoi va handler kich hoat ngay sau thay doi.

2. Phan tich hien trang
- Student, Faculty, Lecturer, Employee va Person dung [Authorize(Roles=...)] hard-code.
- UserManagement va RoleManagement dung policy ManageUsers hard-code.
- Layout kiem tra policy ManageUsers thay vi permission tung action.
- He thong da co Identity role va ASP.NET Core Authorization.

3. Phuong an da de xuat
- Phuong an A: IAuthorizationPolicyProvider + AuthorizationHandler + bang DynamicPermission/RolePermission.
- Phuong an B: Role claim co [Authorize(Policy=...)].
- Phuong an C: MVC ActionFilter doc DB.

4. Phuong an duoc lua chon
Phuong an A. Policy provider tao policy theo Permission:<Controller>.<Action>; handler doc RolePermission va Identity role.
