BAO CAO GIAI DOAN 1 - PHAN TICH DU AN

1. Muc tieu
Khao sat ma nguon BTH10 truoc khi thay doi; xac nhan build, cau truc MVC/EF Core, migration va du lieu SQLite hien co; de xuat lo trinh tich hop Identity.

2. Phan tich
- Project nam trong thu muc BTH10 nhung file project la BTH7.csproj, target net10.0, namespace BTH7.
- MVC voi cac controller Student, Faculty, Lecturer, Employee, Home va Person; giao dien Razor/Bootstrap; cac danh sach CRUD su dung jQuery AJAX modal, tim kiem va phan trang.
- ApplicationDbContext hien ke thua DbContext, dung SQLite; cac bang nghiep vu va quan he Student-Faculty duoc bao toan qua migrations hien co.
- Program.cs moi dang ky MVC va ApplicationDbContext; chua co Identity, cookie authentication, authorization, seed data, email sender hay migration tu dong.
- appsettings.json tro den App.db (Cache=Shared). App.db dang co du lieu: Students 11, Faculties 3, Employees 0, Lecturers 0. Ca 8 migration hien co da duoc ghi nhan trong __EFMigrationsHistory.
- CRUD controllers co ho tro AJAX va form truyen thong; Faculty chan xoa khoa co sinh vien; Student tao StudentCode tu khoa. Layout hien chua co navigation den cac trang nghiep vu.
- PersonController la controller cu truy cap Student, nhung khong co Views/Person; can danh gia route nay khi ap dung authorization de khong de lai duong truy cap bo qua chinh sach.
- Student.AdmissionYear duoc Ignore trong model EF; khong nen thay doi schema nay trong pham vi Identity.
- Khong tim thay test project/test case trong BTH10. Cac file bin/obj va thu vien frontend co san khong duoc xem la ma nguon can sua.

3. Cac van de hien co
- Chua co Identity/Authentication/Authorization; cac action CRUD hien tai chua yeu cau dang nhap.
- Chua co cac trang tai khoan, email sender, confirm/reset token flow, role policies hay seed role/user.
- Build nen thanh cong nhung co 12 warnings: 2 canh bao NU1901 ve NuGet.Packaging/NuGet.Protocol; 8 canh bao nullable tren cac model Student/Lecturer/Employee; 2 canh bao nullable trong Student Details/Delete view.
- Migrations lich su co migration Remove_Phone_Column va AddTrainingSystem rong. Khong sua lai migration cu; them migration Identity theo huong additive.
- Student va Faculty hien co du lieu. Can sao luu va xac nhan ket qua database sau migration; khong duoc dung EnsureCreated thay cho migrations.

4. Ke hoach nang cap de xuat
1) Xac nhan ma tran quyen va quy tac mat khau seed.
2) Giai quyet/cam ket tinh trang nen: giu nguyen schema nghiep vu, xac nhan build va chay app; chi sua loi phat sinh hoac can thiet cho tich hop.
3) Ke thua IdentityDbContext, dang ky Identity/cookie, tao migration Identity toi thieu va cap nhat SQLite an toan.
4) Them Account MVC va view Bootstrap cho dang nhap/dang xuat/dang ky/doi mat khau/quen mat khau/dat lai/xac nhan email/doi email, cung AccessDenied.
5) Them role policies, bao ve controller/action tai server, dong bo lien ket giao dien; xu ly unlock tai khoan neu kich hoat lockout.
6) Seed roles va tai khoan idempotent, khong tao tai khoan trung; dua thong tin nhay cam ra User Secrets/environment variables.
7) Them email sender qua DI: SMTP doc tu configuration; sender gia lap chi cho Development va ghi ro cach lay link kiem thu.
8) Build, ap dung migration tren database hien tai, smoke-test luong auth/authorization; kiem tra lai cac luong CRUD/AJAX/search/pagination va tinh toan ven du lieu.
