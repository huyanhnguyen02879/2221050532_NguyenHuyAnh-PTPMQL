BAO CAO GIAI DOAN 5 - GAN ROLE CHO NGUOI DUNG

1. Muc tieu
Bo sung giao dien Administrator xem tai khoan, Role hien tai va cap nhat nhieu Role bang AJAX, khong reload trang, co validation va bao ve tai khoan Administrator cuoi cung.

2. Phan tich hien trang
- UserManagementController chi hien thi danh sach tai khoan va mo khoa tai khoan.
- UserManager va RoleManager da duoc dang ky trong ASP.NET Core Identity.
- Cac Role seed hien co: Administrator, Manager, Lecturer, Employee, Student.
- Chua co giao dien gán hoac thu hoi Role cho IdentityUser.
- AspNetUserRoles ho tro quan he nhieu-nhieu giua nguoi dung va Role.

3. Phuong an da de xuat
- Phuong an A: user chon nhieu checkbox Role va server thay the tap Role bang UserManager AddToRoles/RemoveFromRoles.
- Phuong an B: moi nguoi dung chi co mot Role duoc luu trong bang Rieng.
- Phuong an C: khong cho thay doi Role va chi dung seed role.

4. Phuong an duoc lua chon
Phuong an A vi tan dung AspNetUserRoles, ho tro nhieu Role va giu nguyen Identity architecture.

9. Thay doi Database
Khong thay doi schema. Thao tac tren AspNetUserRoles:
- Dat Manager va Employee cho tai khoan manager@system.local trong kiem thu.
- Thu hoi Employee va khoi phuc chi con Manager sau kiem thu.
- Khong thay doi Role cua tai khoan Administrator.

10. Cac buoc thuc hien
- Them Roles va UserRoleEditViewModel.
- Them RoleManager vao UserManagementController.
- Hien thi danh sach Role hien tai tren trang tai khoan.
- Them modal checkbox chon nhieu Role.
- Thuc hien AddToRolesAsync/RemoveFromRolesAsync.
- Cap nhat Security Stamp sau thay doi Role de danh sach Role cookie duoc xac thuc lai.
- Chan thu hoi Administrator neu tai khoan hien tai la Administrator cuoi cung.
- Tu choi Role ID khong hop le.
- Loc cac Role ID rong trong form POST.
- Them AJAX modal va fallback MVC view.

11. Ket qua Build
- dotnet build thanh cong sau phat hien va sua loi type IReadOnlyList.
- 0 build error sau build cuoi.
- Cac warning hien tai giong giai doan truoc, khong co loi moi trong controller/view moi.

12. Ket qua kiem thu
- Trang UserManagement render va co nut Cap nhat Role.
- Chua dang nhap van bi redirect denial trang quan ly.
- Thu hoi Role Administrator cua tai khoan cuoi cung tra validation va khong cho phep.
- Gán dong thoi Manager va Employee cho manager@system.local thanh cong.
- SQL xac nhan role sau gán la Employee,Manager.
- Thu hoi Employee thanh cong.
- SQL sau thu hoi chi con Manager.
- Security Stamp duoc cap nhat sau thay doi Role.

13. Cac loi phat hien
- PowerShell form test ban dau gui array sai dinh dang va server tra "Co Role khong hop le".
- Build tam thoi dung vi server dang chay va giu executable.
- Loi nay khong xay ra voi form checkbox browser vi browser gui repeated form fields.

14. Cach khac phuc
- Loc bo Role ID rong va disallow role id khong ton tai.
- Dung repeated form field trong smoke test dung nguon HTML browser.
- Dung stop server truoc dotnet build de tranh lock executable.

15. Luu y cho giai doan tiep theo
- Giai doan tiep theo la phan quyen dong theo Controller/Action voi AuthorizationPolicyProvider va AuthorizationHandler.
- Can chuyen cac [Authorize(Roles=...)] hard-code sang permission DB.
- Bo sung migration cho bang DynamicPermission neu chon luu Controller/Action trong DB.
- Duy tri Administrator high priority va fallback de khong khoa toan bo he thong.
